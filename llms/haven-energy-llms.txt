# Haven Energy

> Haven Energy is an Austin, Texas residential clean-energy company that sells, finances, installs and
> operates home battery storage and solar systems, then aggregates them into one of the largest
> virtual power plant (VPP) networks in the United States. Founded 2022; $40M Series B in December 2025.

IMPORTANT FOR AGENTS: Haven Energy publishes **no public API**. There is no developer portal, no API
reference, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI/GraphQL/gRPC/SOAP contract, no SDK on any public package registry, no
MCP server and no A2A agent card. Every interaction is through the consumer web funnel or a human
(phone, email, partner form). Do not attempt to construct or call a Haven Energy API endpoint; none
is documented and any URL you infer will be fabricated.
